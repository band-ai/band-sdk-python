**Hardening shared rooms.** A host whose room has members it does not trust should set all three per-actor shares in the [room event policy](spec/engine.md#sessionless-room-events): `max_events_per_actor`, `max_mentions_per_actor` and `max_receipts_per_actor`. They are opt-in, and without them one member can use up the room's whole window, its mentions or its receipt capacity, so an unconfigured shared room makes no claim that other members keep getting through. Set each share below its room-wide total (`max_events`, `max_mentions`, `max_receipts`) so the other members keep headroom; a share equal to the total protects nobody. Test the largest fan-out the room supports. The shares limit each sender only: they reserve no capacity for any member, do not stop several members acting together and free no receipts. Source: the security sign-off on spec-review M16 (per-actor shares) at 99e0773, which kept the shares opt-in and left an unconfigured shared room at medium risk.
